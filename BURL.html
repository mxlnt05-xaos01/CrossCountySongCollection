Burlington County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1694 - One of two original counties in West Jersey

County Seat: Mount Holly

Name Origin: The old ancient name for an inland market near Bridlington, England

Political Affiliation: Majority Democratic

Part of: NJ Districts 1 & 3

Major Highways: I-295, I-95/NJTP, US 130, US 206

Notes: Top 2 Largest Historic Counties in NJ

Music: Amidst the Hotspots (Burlington County)
