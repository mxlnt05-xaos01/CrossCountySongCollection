Cape May County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1692 - Lower Southeastern Separation from Burlington County

County Seat: Cape May Court House

Name Origin: Named from a 17th C Dutch Explorer Cornelius Jacobsen Mey, who explored and surveyed the Delaware Bay to the south of the county

Political Affiliation: Republican Stronghold

Major Highways: Garden State Parkway, US 9

Notes: Lewes-Cape May Ferry Station
