Hudson County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1840 - Penisular Separation from Bergen County

County Seat: Jersey City

Name Origin: The English explorer Henry Hudson (d. 1611), who explored portions of New Jersey's coastline

Political Affiliation: Democratic Stronghold

Major Highways: I-280, I-95/NJTP, US 1-9, I-78, NJ 440, NJ 3, NJ 495

Notes: Highest population density of all NJ counties due to tiny size
