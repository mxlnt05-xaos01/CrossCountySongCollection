Hunterdon County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1714 - Northwestern Half Separation from Burlington County

County Seat: Flemington

Name Origin: Robert Hunter (1664-1734), the Colonial Governor of New Jersey from 1710 to 1720

Political Affiliation: Republican Stronghold

Part of: NJ District 7

Major Highways: US 202, US 22, I-78

Notes: First Northwestern County of West Jersey
