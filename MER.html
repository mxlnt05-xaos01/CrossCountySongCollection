Mercer County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1838 - Formed following the partition between Burlington, Hunterdon, Middlesex, and Somerset Counties

County Seat: Trenton

Name Origin: The Continental Army General Hugh Mercer (1726-1777), who died at the Battle of Princeton

Political Affiliation: Democratic Stronghold

Major Highways: I-295, US 1, US 206, US 130, NJ 133, NJ 33

Notes: Historic Universities Hub (i.e. Princeton University, Rider University, The College of New Jersey), Trenton Transit Center
