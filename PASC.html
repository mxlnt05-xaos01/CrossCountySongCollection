Passaic County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1837 - Formed following the partition between Bergen and Essex Counties

County Seat: Paterson

Name Origin: The Lenape word 'Pasaeck', meaning "valley"

Political Affiliation: Affiliations Tied

Part of: NJ Districts 5, 9, & 11

Major Highways: I-80, I-287, NJ 3, NJ 19, NJ 21, US 46, US 202, Garden State Parkway

Notes: Panhandle Shape
