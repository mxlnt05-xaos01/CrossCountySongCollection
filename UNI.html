Union County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1857 - Lower Northern Central Separation from Essex County

County Seat: Elizabeth

Name Origin: The union of the United States, which was being threatened by the dispute over slavery

Political Affiliation: Democratic Stronghold

Part of: NJ Districts 7, 10, & 12

Major Highways: I-95/NJTP, I-78, US 1-9, I-278, US 22, NJ 439, Garden State Parkway

Notes: Jersey Gardens Mall, IKEA, Northeastern Corridor

Music: Fields of Diverse Cultures (Union County)
