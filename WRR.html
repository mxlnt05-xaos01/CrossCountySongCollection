Warren County

Info:

Date of Formation: 1824 - Half Separation from Sussex County

County Seat: Belvidere

Name Origin: The American Revolutionary War General Joseph Warren (1741-1775), killed at the Battle of Bunker Hill

Political Affiliation: Majority Republican

Part of: NJ District 7

Major Highways: I-78, I-80, US 22, US 46, NJ 57, NJ 94

Notes: Mount Tammany, The Kittatinny Mountains, Hiking Trails

Music: The Very Foundation of The Mountain Ranges (Warren County)
